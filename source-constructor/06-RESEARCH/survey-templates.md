# Source Constructor (SOC) — Survey Templates

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-02
**Status:** Approved
**Owner:** Research Lead

---

## Overview

This document contains survey templates used at different stages of the product lifecycle:

1. **Pre-Launch Survey** — Validate problem and pricing
2. **Onboarding Survey** — Understand new user context
3. **NPS Survey** — Measure loyalty and satisfaction
4. **Feature Prioritization Survey** — Guide roadmap decisions
5. **Exit Survey** — Capture churn reasons

---

## Survey 1: Pre-Launch Validation Survey

**Distribution:** Landing page waitlist form, social media, partner communities
**Goal:** Validate problem severity and willingness to pay before building
**Target:** 200+ responses

---

- E. Other: ___________

**Distribution:** In-app announcement banner; email to active users

*(Single choice)*

- Project Generation (structured plans)
- Daily AI Companion (check-ins)
- Progress Tracking (milestones)
- Insights Dashboard (patterns over time)

---

**Q2. Which feature do you find LEAST useful right now?**
*(Same options as Q1)*
