Typography

Inter typeface — complete type scale

Display — 48px Bold
Heading 1 — 36px Bold
Heading 2 — 28px Semibold
Heading 3 — 22px Semibold
Heading 4 — 18px Semibold
Body Large — 18px Regular. Used for lead text and introductory paragraphs that need extra emphasis.
Body — 16px Regular. Default body text for all content areas. Maintain a maximum line length of 65–75 characters for optimal readability.
Body Small — 14px Regular. Used for captions, helper text, and secondary information.
CAPTION — 12px Medium Uppercase. Used for metadata, timestamps, labels.
