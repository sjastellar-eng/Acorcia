## The Problem

92% of people who set goals abandon them within 90 days. This isn't a motivation problem — it's a **clarity problem**. Most people are pursuing goals that don't actually reflect what they want. They're chasing what sounds good, what peers are doing, or what they think they *should* want.

The result: side projects that die, career transitions that stall, ideas that never become anything.

---

## The Solution

**Source Constructor** is an AI companion that conducts deep, conversational Discovery Sessions to help users uncover their true desires — then automatically generates a structured project with a 90-day roadmap and daily accountability system.

**Three-step process:**

1. **Discover** — A 25–35 minute AI conversation that surfaces genuine motivations through Socratic questioning
2. **Structure** — Automatic generation of a personalized project plan (phases, milestones, weekly tasks)
3. **Maintain** — Daily 3-minute AI check-ins that maintain momentum through pattern detection and gentle accountability
